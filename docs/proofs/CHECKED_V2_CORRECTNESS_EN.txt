V2 WITH CHECKED PREDICTIONS: CORRECTNESS WITHOUT AN EXTREMAL HYPOTHESIS
24 October 2026. Mathematical argument accompanying the isolated release.

1. Scope

The common-leaf-level global maximum conjecture remains OPEN. It is not
proved by this document or by successful finite computations. Instead we
prove that it is unnecessary for acceptance of the checked implementation.
The tight prediction becomes an enforced contract on every consumed value.
A bad contract rejects before unsafe use; a retry uses an unconditional cap.

This argument uses the original exact transfer, the proved V0/V1 and mixed
V2 identities, and the separately proved boundary functional. Their existing
proofs are included as explicit prerequisites. It is not a machine-checked
verification of the whole Python/Numba toolchain. The scalar-audited domain
is 1<=n<=34. Completion requires sufficient RAM; a guard failure returns no
accepted answer. No new full M32-M34 computation was performed here.

2. An unconditional V1 bound

Let P,Q be ballot prefixes of lengths a,b and common height h. Write
P=D0 '(' D1 ... '(' Dh, Q=E0 '(' ... '(' Eh. Put p=(a-h)/2, q=(b-h)/2,
k=p+q. There are p,q closed vertices and root profiles u_i,v_i, with
m_i=u_i+v_i and sum m_i<=k. Let r_i be the sum of root degrees,
rho_i=root count+r_i, sigma=sum(degree+1)^2 over all closed vertices.
Set K=product binom(u_i+v_i,u_i)<=2^k.

The proved V1 formula (THEOREM_E1_GR.md) has the following normalized bounds:
 sigma(P)+sigma(Q) <= p^2+3p+q^2+3q <= k^2+3k;
 sum [v_i*rho_i(P)/(u_i+1)+u_i*rho_i(Q)/(v_i+1)] <= k+2pq;
 sum 2(m_i+1)[u_i/(v_i+2)+v_i/(u_i+2)] <= k^2+k;
 its adjacent-level sum <= 2h+2k+pq.

The first uses sum degrees<=number of closed vertices. For the second use
sum r_i(P)<=p, sum r_i(Q)<=q, v_i<=q, u_i<=p, and u_i/(u_i+1)<=1.
The third uses both denominators>=2 and sum m_i<=k. For the last use
(m_{i-1}+1)/(u_{i-1}+1)<=v_{i-1}+1 and
(m_i+1)/(v_i+1)<=u_i+1, together with their interchanged versions.
The resulting cross terms count P-root/Q-root pairs at adjacent levels,
each pair at most once; hence their sum<=pq.
Thus V1/K<=2k^2+7k+3pq+2h<=3k^2+8k+2h<=4(k+h+1)^2, so

 V1(P,Q) <= 4(k+h+1)^2 * 2^k.                         (A)

No restriction to leaves or maximizing profiles occurs here.

3. Unconditional V2 cap

Let J2=T1 V1+T2 V0 be the incoming positive-excess source. The mixed-DD
reverse T1 images are indexed by terminal roots (at most k total) and have
parameters (k,h+1). There are at most two ending-open T1 images, with
parameters (k+1,h-1). T2 has at most one image, with (k+2,h-1), if h>0.
Since V0<=2^k, a deliberately loose bound is

 J2 <= [8(k+2)(k+h+3)^2+4] * 2^k.                     (B)

Unroll V2=J2+T0 V2 backwards. A zero-excess step either removes two final
opens, taking (k,h) to (k,h-1), or removes a final close on one side and
appends an open on the other, taking (k,h) to (k-1,h+1). There are at most
two choices of the latter kind. A state with two final opens has only the
former, deterministic step. Invalid/forbidden states terminate a path.

Fix a binary word of d close-removal choices. There are at most 2^d such
words. After each word the deterministic open-removal chain has at most
h+k+1 states. On that chain k'=k-d and k'+h'<=k+h. The polynomial factor
in (B) cannot increase and 2^k'=2^(k-d). Sum J2 over recursion-tree NODES,
not just complete histories. The d=0,...,k groups yield

 V2(P,Q) <= Uc(a,b,h), where
 Uc=(k+1)(k+h+1)[8(k+2)(k+h+3)^2+4] * 2^k.             (C)

This proves the coarse bound for arbitrary finite shapes, without an
extremal hypothesis. It is intentionally looser than the fast prediction.

4. Exact reconstruction and signed arithmetic

The existing E2 theorem proves, for actual profile anchors P0,Q0,
 V2(P,Q)=V2(P,Q0)+V2(P0,Q)-V2(P0,Q0)
          +(f(P)-f(P0))^T H(u,v)(f(Q)-f(Q0)),          (D)
where f=(s,r_0,...,r_h), s=sigma-sum roots. Here H_ss=K,
H_{rP_i,s}=K*v_i/(u_i+1), H_{s,rQ_j}=K*u_j/(v_j+1),
H_{rP_i,rQ_i}=K, and the off-diagonal entries are products of the
corresponding ratios times K. One-sided full-shape functions cancel in
(D); this is a proved identity, not empirical interpolation.

Put Tp=p(p+3), Tq=q(q+3), Kmax=binom(p+q,p). At fixed profiles,
|delta sP|<=Tp and sum |delta rP_i|<=2p, and likewise on Q. The native
signed contraction has the conservative bounds
 Amax=Kmax*(Tp+2pq),
 Nmax=(Amax+Kmax*p*q)*p,
 Bmax=Nmax+Kmax*p,
 Mmax=Amax*Tq+2q*Bmax.
Amax bounds the first contraction; Nmax its subsequent division numerators;
Bmax every resulting root coefficient; Mmax the sum of absolute mixed
terms, including all partial sums. K*v_i/(u_i+1)<=Kmax*q; replacing any
denominator by 1 only loosens these bounds. When p=0 all P differences
vanish and the decoder takes its direct branch.

If validated anchor sections are in [0,U], checking
 max(Amax,Nmax,Bmax,3U+2Mmax)<2^62                       (E)
keeps reconstruction within signed int64. This requires NO assertion that
all virtual V2 entries<=U. Every requested rectangle AND every requested
pair must have exact divisions, nonnegative final value and value<=U,
BEFORE assignment to the unsigned output dtype. Nonintegrality/negative
values are hard errors. A cap violation is PredictionRejected. A rejected
buffer is never returned to a consumer.

Cross sections are obtained by the original positive transfer. For parent
addition the kernel tests old>=0, parent<=U and old<=U-parent BEFORE adding.
The complete signed sample buffer is checked before narrowing its storage.
Source adoption checks its actual maximum before any narrower conversion.
An independent source-range check, executed once per compact block, uses
 Jcert2=max(4k(k+h+2)^2*2^k
           +[h>0]*(8(k+h+1)^2*2^(k+1)+2^(k+2)),
           6(k+2)*2^(k+2)).
The first term follows from (A), applied separately to the k terminal-root
images, two ending-open images and the T2 image. The last term also covers
coefficient planes under the incoming-source proof. Checking 2*Jcert2<2^61
before source evaluation keeps R+C and R+R'-K in signed range even for U=0.
The unsigned source/table operations retain their separate domain bounds;
the scalar audit additionally checks Jcert*(n+3)^4<2^64. The incoming-source
identities and coefficient-plane bounds are explicit prerequisites, not
conclusions inferred from a successful Mn comparison.

5. Induction on consumed values

Initially the stored empty-state value is exact. Assume ordinary stored
values are exact and every already returned compact value is exact within
its declared interval. Nonnegative transfers to ordinary targets are
bounded by declared predecessor caps times exact map multiplicities, plus
proved analytic source caps. Their selected dtype therefore cannot overflow.
An unrequested virtual compact entry is not an operand of this transfer.

For a new compact block the analytic source is exact. Parent requests either
return exact capped values or abort. Checked additions and pre-cast checks
therefore make each successfully stored anchor sample exact within its cap.
For a subsequent read, (D) and (E) give the exact integer before the cap test.
It is either returned exactly within the cap or rejected before use. This
proves the invariant layer by layer, including lazy reads and pair reads.

The proved boundary functional consumes only returned exact values; its
omitted OO rectangle is separately proved unobservable. Its final integer
sum is consequently the original four-move transfer Mn. Returning U from
compact maximum_reduce() is valid as an ENFORCED ACCESS CONTRACT for
planning. It is not a measured or proved maximum of the entire virtual block.
Thus successful acceptance does not require the global maximum conjecture.

6. Recovery and limits

On PredictionRejected the private stores/caches of that attempt are destroyed
and the calculation starts again from the initial state with Uc in (C).
It never resumes from a rejected partial layer. Either successful attempt
is exact by section 5. Bound (C) prevents a valid proved-cap attempt from
failing merely because the maximum conjecture is false. A wider dtype or
additional RAM may be required in this EXCEPTIONAL retry. Resource failure
returns no accepted answer. The normal predicted path retains the same
array dimensions, coefficient widths and transfer count as the baseline.

Orders above 34 require renewed index/width/signature audits. Python -O/-OO
is rejected because inherited invariants still contain assertions. No new
Linux/server performance claim is made for this isolated release. The proof
is not a retroactive independent CRT certificate for M32-M34. M31 already
has its separately delivered independent CRT certificate.
