EXACT FACTORIZATION OF THE EXCESS-TWO TREE RESPONSES
AND ITS APPLICATION TO THE EXCESS-THREE SOURCE
13 October 2026

Scope. These identities follow algebraically from the previously established
J2 source and forest Green operator. Finite tests are additional checks, not
their proof. No extremal hypothesis is used in this new implementation.
No claim of bibliographic priority is made.

1. A closed response for an arbitrary rooted tree

For a rooted plane tree T let d be its root degree, let its ordered children
be T1,...,Td, and put sigma(T)=sum over its vertices of (degree+1)^2.
Associate four integers (sigma,d,U,V) with T. A leaf has (1,0,0,0).
For child values (sigma_j,d_j,U_j,V_j), define

 A=sum_j(sigma_j-1), D=sum_j d_j,
 t=d(d+1)/2, s2=d(d+1)(2d+1)/6,
 cross=sum_{j<k}(sigma_j-1)(sigma_k-1),
 sigma=(d+1)^2+sum_j sigma_j,
 U=sum_j(U_j+V_j)+(d^2+2d-1)A+(3d+4)D+cross
     +(8t^2+27s2+49t)/6,
 V=(d+1)sigma+D-s2-1.

The numerator in the last term of U is divisible by six: it equals
sum_{j=1}^d(8j^3+27j^2+49j), whose summands are divisible by six.

Take ordered ballot bases A0,B0 at equal height i, with root profiles u,v,
terminal counts x=u_i,y=v_i, and L=product_{l<i} binom(u_l+v_l,u_l).
Let
 K=L binom(x+y,x), KA=L binom(x+y+1,x+1),
 KB=L binom(x+y+1,x), KAB=L binom(x+y+2,x+1),
 KAy=L binom(x+y+1,x+2).
Here VA=V1(A0+'()',B0), VB=V1(A0,B0+'()'), and W=V1(A0,B0).
Let C_T be the sum of J2 along the deterministic interior reverse path
of T above these bases, excluding the ending base state. Then exactly

 C_T=(sigma-d-1)W+(d+1)(VA+KAB)+VB
       +K U+KA V+KB(sigma-1)+KAy d(d+1)/2.                 (1)

This is a seven-coordinate tree response. The coordinates are
(sigma-d-1,d+1,1,U,V,sigma-1,t). Child permutation does not change them.
Permutation of roots in the external forests can still change V2.

2. Inductive proof of (1)

Write F_k=T1...Tk, alpha_j=sigma_j-1, A_k=sum_{j<=k}alpha_j,
D_k=sum_{j<=k}d_j, and t_k=k(k+1)/2.
The interior of T decomposes without overlap into the d+1 boundary states
(A0+'('+F_k,B0+'('), k=0,...,d), and each child's interior excursion,
with bases (A0+'('+F_{j-1},B0+'(').

Substitution in the proved J2 inverse-move formula gives the following
boundary-state source:

 J_k=k W+VA+KAB+1[k=0]VB
       +KA(A_k+k(k+3)+t_k)+KB k+KAy k
       +K(k A_k+2D_k+(4k^3+12k^2+20k)/6).                (2)

To verify the inverse-root part of this substitution explicitly, for root j
put w=k-j. Its V1 inverse image has the same base contributions W, and
its additional contribution is
 KA*j+KB+K(j^2+j+2d_j+4+A_k+w(w+3)).
Summing over j gives k W+KA*t_k+KB*k and the last K term of (2).
The Q-ending-open inverse adds VA+KA(A_k+k(k+3))+KAy*k;
the P-ending-open inverse occurs only at k=0 and adds VB;
the excess-two inverse adds KAB. This proves (2) directly.

For a child j put a=j-1 and A_<j=sum_{l<j}alpha_l, D_<j=sum_{l<j}d_l.
The three V1 quantities appearing in its inductive response are

 W_j=W+KA*j+KB+K(j^2-1+A_<j),
 VA_j=W+KA*(j+1)+KB+K(j(j+2)+A_<j),
 VB_j=j W+KA*t_j+KB*(j+1)
         +K(loc(j-1,1)+j*A_<j+D_<j),

where loc(j-1,1)=(4j^3+9j^2+11j-6)/6. Its local binomials are
K_j=K, KA_j=K, KB_j=K*j, KAB_j=K*(j+1), KAy_j=0.
All formulas follow from the exact V1 leaf recurrence and its affine
shape terms; no V2 values are assumed.

Add (2) for k=0,...,d and (1) for each child with these local quantities.
The coefficients of W, VA+KAB, VB, KB and KAy become respectively
sigma-d-1, d+1, 1, sigma-1, t.
The KA coefficient is
(d+1)A+D+2s2+5t+d=(d+1)sigma+D-s2-1=V.
In the K coefficient each alpha_j has coefficient d^2+2d-1, each d_j
has coefficient 3d+4, and the mixed terms give cross. The shape-free
summand at child index j is (8j^3+27j^2+49j)/6. Adding child U_j+V_j
therefore gives exactly U above. This proves the induction. The leaf
case is J2(A0+'(',B0+'(')=VA+VB+KAB, also exactly (1).

3. An explicit ordered feature factorization of the entire V2 coefficient

For a prefix P, retain its forests at levels 0,...,h and their root order.
For root r at level i put alpha=sigma(T)-1, beta=d(T), and let S_before
be the sum of alpha for all earlier roots (earlier levels included).
Let R_before,l be the sum of beta at earlier roots of level l.
Append to Phi_u(P) the following 9+2i integers for this root:

 alpha,beta,U,V,beta(beta+1)/2,alpha*S_before,beta*S_before,
 (alpha*R_before,l,beta*R_before,l) for l=0,...,i.

Thus dim Phi_u=sum_i u_i(9+2i); it depends on the root profile and is
not a fixed constant. Leaf slots vanish and are stored sparsely.
Let f(P)=(sum_roots alpha, sum_level0 beta,...,sum_levelh beta).
The old proved mixed interaction H(u,v) is unchanged.

Insert (1) and the explicit J2 source into the forest Green formula.
Evaluate the Q side first at the all-leaf mathematical anchor. Boundary
sources are affine in the P shape data. In root excursions, the V1 base
terms multiply alpha or beta of the active root; this produces exactly
the ordered alpha*S_before, beta*S_before, alpha*R_before,l and
beta*R_before,l slots above. The remaining terms of (1) are linear in
alpha,beta,U,V,t. Q leaf excursions add only affine P terms. Consequently

 V2(P,Q)=B(u,v)+Phi_u(P).Gamma(u,v)+Phi_v(Q).Gamma(v,u)
                  +f(P)^T H(u,v) f(Q).                 (3)

B is V2 at the all-leaf anchors; these anchors are evaluated at their
own lengths. Gamma is constructed by the finite weighted sums in
factor_math.kernel, not by fitting observed coefficients. profile_kernel
is its compiled integer implementation. The mixed term is justified by
the previous generic mixed theorem, including different-length anchors.
Equation (3) is therefore an identity for arbitrary ordered trees, not
only the tested orders. Neither known Mn nor sampled V2 coefficients
are inputs to this formula.

4. Application to excess three: what has and has not been factored

The proved source is J3=T1 V2+T2 V1. T1 consists of the two ending-open
inverse images and one inverse image for each terminal root on each
side; T2 is the simultaneous closing of both prefixes when h>0.
Substituting (3) factors each T1 summand. Grouping target rows by root
profile allows the constant and Phi coefficients to be accumulated
over the inverse Q images once, then contracted with each P row.
The symmetrical half supplies the inverse P images. T2 V1 keeps its
already proved expression. The e4 closing source T2 V2 also uses (3).

The experimental full program evaluates V3=G[J3] using the retained
zero-excess updates of its e3 arrays. It removes every stored e2 array.
It DOES NOT remove e3 arrays or establish a fixed low-rank closed V3
representation. The old feature-vector obstruction for V3 remains.
For small row-profile groups, an equivalent collapsed root-grid Green
recurrence applies (1) directly and avoids overly wide factor planes.
Both implementations are compiled Numba kernels, not Python loops
over the numerical coefficient grids.

An additional exact factorization avoids repeating V2 for every inverse
root. Write Q=B0+T_j+tail, where B0 contains all earlier terminal roots
and T_j='('+children_j+')'. Let R be the root enclosing tail and let
W be the root whose children are children_j followed by R. Then

 I_j(Q)=B0+'('+interior(W),
 V2(P+'(',I_j(Q))=V2(P,B0)+C_W(B0,P).                    (4)

Proof. At the added level P has no closed roots. Until that level has
been exhausted, its zero-excess reverse history is unique. Its visited
states are precisely the interior excursion of W. The final removal
of the two trailing opens returns to (P,B0). Expansion of the V2
recurrence is therefore the base value plus that excursion's J2 sum.
This proves (4), including the order of the arguments in C_W.

All base values V2(P,B0) in (4) are entries of a single terminal root
grid for P,Q. They are computed together by the collapsed Green
recurrence. The response C_W is given by (1). Thus the inverse-root
sum uses one V2 root grid, rather than a fresh V2 evaluation per root.

W's four statistics require no traversal of children_j. For T_j's
(sigma,d,U,V), recover
 Achild=sigma-(d+1)^2-d,
 Bchild=V-(d+1)sigma+d(d+1)(2d+1)/6+1.
For the appended R=(sR,dR,UR,VR), alphaR=sR-1, put
 F(d)=(8t_d^2+27s2_d+49t_d)/6. Direct subtraction of the parent
recurrence before and after appending R gives

 sigmaW=sigma+2d+3+sR, dW=d+1,
 UW=U+UR+VR+(2d+3)Achild+3Bchild
       +(d^2+4d+2)alphaR+(3d+7)dR+Achild*alphaR
       +F(d+1)-F(d),
 VW=(d+2)sigmaW+Bchild+dR-(d+1)(d+2)(2d+3)/6-1.

Suffix sums of alpha, beta, U+V, and pairwise alpha products give
each enclosing-tail root R by the same parent recurrence. All R and
W statistics therefore take linear work in the number of terminal
roots after their constituent tree statistics have been built.
The closing boundary contribution when P ends in an unmatched open
is evaluated separately as V2(P[:-1],Q+')'). The original proved
T2 V1 source supplies the simultaneous-closing contribution.
fused_half.py implements these identities. The experiment compares
using (4) for small groups with using it for every group, in complete
computations; it does not infer a speedup from the algebra alone.

5. Arithmetic certificates and experimental range

The full trial program accepts 4<=n<=22. Every source operation is
integral; higher-field coefficients use the existing checked unsigned
widths or arbitrary-precision integers. No floating-point interpolation.
Before a target is allocated, its bound is the sum of actual predecessor
maxima times inverse multiplicities, plus the actual exact source maximum.
The new code does not use the conjectured tight global e2 maximum.

For a queried V2 pair with m closed vertices in total and height h, a
conservative rigorous bound used by the direct kernel is

 D2(m,h)=(2m+h)*2^m*
          [(m+2)*4*(m+h+2)^2*2^(m+1)+2^(m+2)].

Indeed, along a zero-excess reverse path m'<=m and m'+h'<=m+h. There
are at most m+2 excess-one inverse images, each bounded by the proved
V1 bound 4*(m+h+2)^2*2^(m+1), plus a V0 term bounded by 2^(m+2).
The Green weighted nonempty visits are at most (2m+h)*2^m. Since all
actual direct-source and tree-response terms are nonnegative, this also
bounds their accumulated partial sums. The sum over mapped inverse
images is checked against its multiplicity times D2 before execution.
Source3 is checked separately before its unsigned accumulation.

For the profile-only kernel put N=sum_i(u_i+v_i) and A=N+h+4.
The profile coefficient bound used is 64*A^5*2^N. In the Green expansion,
the product of a reaching weight and its local root-shuffle binomial is
at most the completed profile shuffle count, hence at most 2^N.
Appending one or two leaf roots multiplies the local binomial by at most
A or A^2. The affine leaf V1 quantities, divided by their shuffle count,
are bounded by 4*A^2. At a boundary source the inverse-root part has
at most N terms; its normalized affine part is bounded by 5*A^3.
The two possible ending-open terms add at most 8*A^3, and the closing
V0 term at most A^2. For leaf root excursions and their feature
coefficients, substitution in (1) gives smaller bounds. Using 32*A^3
uniformly therefore bounds each normalized boundary/excursion
contribution;
the collapsed grids have at most 3pq+2(p+q)+h+1 <= A^2 contributions.
Copying a basic contribution into earlier root slots does not multiply
a single slot's value. Summing gives 32*A^5*2^N; the implemented factor
64 also covers primitive coefficient numerators and partial sums before
exact divisions. The admissible N<=20, h<=30 domain is checked first.
Actual coefficient-times-feature L1 bounds are then checked separately
before sparse contractions; the profile-only bound is not used to
license arbitrary shape-feature products.
The recorded certificates, target checks, and full coefficient comparisons
are included with the measurements. Windows commit limits and the system
memory reserve stay active. These certificates do not extend the audited
experimental implementation automatically beyond n=22.

The performance result belongs in REPORT_GR.txt; algebraic factorization
alone is not evidence of a speedup in a complete Mn computation.
