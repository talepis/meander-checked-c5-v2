This candidate package does not assert discovery priority for every value.
Existing work by Jensen, Howroyd and subsequent researchers must be cited
when submitting new terms. Recheck OEIS and the current literature on the
actual submission date; earlier availability can change. In particular,
a successful independent modular audit supports arithmetic confidence but
is not a claim that the sequence term has never been published.

The local derivation and exact implementation prerequisites are supplied in
docs/proofs. Formal mathematical attribution must be completed by the human
author before journal, preprint or OEIS submission. Historical performance
claims compare particular machines and source versions; they are not
portable theoretical complexity bounds.
